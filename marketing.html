One app for the whole community

NB SAI brings everything a Sai Baba community organization runs on into a single, beautifully simple iOS app — seva sign-ups, SSE classes, devotional sessions, donations, and announcements.

Built for three roles

Members — sign up for volunteer activities, RSVP for events, donate, and stay connected with the community.
Teachers (SSE) — post resources for your group, mark attendance, and keep private notes shared only with co-Teachers.
Officers — publish announcements, post seva sign-up sheets, upload rosters, and coordinate across the four tiles: Service, SSE, Devotion, General.

What's inside

  • Volunteer sign-ups with live progress bars — never wonder if a seva is full.
  • SSE for four age groups — Little Lights, Junior Devotees, Youth Seekers, Young Adults — each with their own teacher, schedule, and resources.
  • Bhajan and study-circle calendar — sign up to lead, join as a co-singer, or pick a study topic.
  • Donations through six payment methods — PayPal, Apple Pay, Venmo, Zelle, ACH, or card. Tax-deductible receipt emailed instantly.
  • Events RSVP for bhajans, festivals, and classes.
  • Private and secure — no ads, no trackers, no third-party data sales. Privacy Policy.

Free, with no ads

NB SAI is free to download and free to use. We accept donations to the Organization through the in-app donation flow.
